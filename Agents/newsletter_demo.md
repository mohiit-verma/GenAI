## [OpenAI Newsletter] - September 27th, 2023

### What's New at OpenAI?

#### Sora 2 System Card
Sora 2 is our newest state-of-the-art video and audio generation model. Building on the foundation of Sora, this new model introduces capabilities that were previously difficult for prior video models to achieve – including more accurate physics, sharper realism, synchronized audio, enhanced steerability, and a range of other improvements. For more information, check out the [Sora 2 System Card](https://openai.com/index/sora-2-system-card).

#### Launching Sora Responsibly
To address the novel safety challenges posed by a state-of-the-art video model as well as a new social creation platform, we've built Sora 2 and the Sora app with safety at the foundation. This approach includes concrete protections designed to safeguard users and creators alike. For more details, visit the [Launching Sora Responsibly](https://openai.com/index/launching-sora-responsibly) page.

#### Argentina’s AI Opportunity
Argentina has an exciting opportunity with its first Stargate project—a collaboration between OpenAI and Sur Energy that could transform the country into a leading player in artificial intelligence, sustainable infrastructure, and digital innovation. Learn more about this groundbreaking initiative at [Argentina’s AI Opportunity](https://openai.com/global-affairs/argentinas-ai-opportunity).

#### OpenAI and Broadcom Announce Strategic Collaboration
OpenAI and Broadcom are collaborating to deploy 10 gigawatts of OpenAI-designed AI accelerators. These accelerators will be used to power scalable, energy-efficient AI infrastructure, aiming to reach 1 gigawatt by 2026. Stay updated on this significant advancement at [OpenAI and Broadcom Announce Strategic Collaboration](https://openai.com/index/openai-and-broadcom-announce-strategic-collaboration).

#### Codex Now Generally Available
Codex is now generally available with powerful new features for developers, including a Slack integration, Codex SDK, and admin tools like usage dashboards and workspace management. Learn more about these enhancements at [Codex is now generally available](https://openai.com/index/codex-now-generally-available).

#### AMD and OpenAI Announce Partnership
AMD and OpenAI are working together to deploy 6 gigawatts of AMD Instinct GPUs, starting with 1 gigawatt in 2026. This partnership aims to accelerate global AI innovation and drive scalability and efficiency. Explore this exciting development at [AMD and OpenAI Announce Strategic Partnership](https://openai.com/index/openai-amd-strategic-partnership).

#### Sora Feed Philosophy
Discover the Sora feed philosophy, which focuses on sparking creativity, fostering connections, and keeping experiences safe with personalized recommendations, parental controls, and strong guardrails. Learn more at [The Sora feed philosophy](https://openai.com/index/sora-feed-philosophy).

#### Introducing AgentKit
AgentKit is a new tool designed to help developers transition from prototypes to production environments faster. Expanded evals capabilities and reinforcement fine-tuning for agents are included. Check out the [Introducing AgentKit](https://openai.com/index/introducing-agentkit) announcement for more details.

#### OpenAI Partnerships and Initiatives
Join us as we continue to collaborate with various organizations to promote AI adoption and ensure responsible use. Visit the [Partnering with AARP](https://openai.com/index/aarp-partnership-older-adults-online-safety), [Combating Online Child Sexual Exploitation & Abuse](https://openai.com/index/combating-online-child-sexual-exploitation-abuse), and [Accelerating AI Adoption in Europe](https://openai.com/global-affairs/accelerating-ai-uptake-in-europe) pages for further updates.

# AI News Roundup

In this week's round-up, we highlight the latest developments in the field of artificial intelligence, including breakthroughs in natural language processing, advances in machine learning, and innovative applications of AI technology.

## Natural Language Processing

- **BERT Updates**: Researchers at Google released BERT Plus, an update to the popular pre-trained language model, focusing on enhancing understanding of context and semantics in text. This update promises improved performance in downstream tasks like question answering and summarization.

- **Language Models for Medical Diagnosis**: Scientists at Stanford University developed a deep learning model called MedQA that can diagnose medical conditions based on patient narratives. This model shows promise in aiding healthcare professionals in diagnosing diseases accurately.

## Machine Learning

- **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**: A paper published in Nature Communications introduced a new type of GAN architecture called Generative Flow Network, which offers better control over the generated samples compared to traditional GANs. This could lead to more versatile and efficient generative models.

- **Quantum Computing in Machine Learning**: Researchers at IBM demonstrated the application of quantum computing in machine learning using Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA). This opens up new possibilities for solving complex optimization problems much faster than classical algorithms.

## Innovative Applications

- **AI-Powered Personal Assistants**: Companies like Amazon and Microsoft are integrating AI assistants directly into their products, offering users more seamless interactions with smart devices and services. These assistants learn user preferences and provide personalized recommendations.

- **AI in Agriculture**: Farmers are increasingly turning to AI-driven decision-making tools to optimize crop yields and manage resources efficiently. Technologies like drones equipped with sensors and machine learning algorithms are helping farmers predict weather patterns and monitor soil health.

These developments underscore the rapid progress being made in the field of AI, pushing boundaries in areas like natural language processing, machine learning, and practical applications. As AI continues to evolve, we look forward to seeing even more impactful innovations in the coming years.

## Emerging Technologies

- **Edge Computing**: Edge computing is gaining traction as a solution for handling large amounts of data locally rather than relying solely on cloud servers. This reduces latency and enhances security while increasing computational power.

- **Blockchain Technology**: Blockchain continues to revolutionize industries ranging from finance to supply chain management. Innovations like decentralized exchanges and secure identity verification platforms are paving the way for more transparent and secure transactions.

## Disruptive Innovations

- **Autonomous Vehicles**: Self-driving cars are advancing rapidly, with companies like Waymo and Tesla leading the charge. These vehicles not only offer convenience but also contribute to reducing traffic congestion and carbon emissions.
