## Generative AI in depth: A survey of recent advances, model ...
- Generative AI Advances**: Recent studies highlight the pivotal role of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) in creating realistic images, text, and audio that closely mimic real-world data. These techniques leverage neural networks to generate new samples from existing datasets, expanding the capabilities of machine learning for creative applications.
- Deep Learning's Impact**: Deep learning has significantly advanced generative models through advancements in architectures like Transformers and large-scale pre-trained models, enabling more sophisticated and nuanced generation tasks across various domains such as language modeling, image synthesis, and music composition.
- Model Diversity Explored**: Research indicates that different types of generative models offer distinct advantages depending on the task at hand. For instance, GANs excel in style transfer and domain adaptation due to their ability to create highly realistic yet stylized outputs, while VAEs are favored for their probabilistic nature, allowing for better control over the generated output distribution. DMs represent an emerging trend, offering a balance between generality and efficiency by using diffusion processes to sample from initial noise distributions into desired final states.
- Global Perspective**: The field continues to evolve globally with international collaborations and shared research initiatives fostering innovation and standardization across methodologies and technologies used in generative AI development. This collaborative effort ensures continuous improvement and broad applicability of these powerful tools.
- Future Directions**: Looking ahead, ongoing developments include improvements in training efficiency, increased scalability, and integration of generative models with other AI systems to enhance overall system performance and functionality. Additionally, there is growing interest in ethical considerations and responsible deployment of these advanced AI technologies.

## From AI pilots to AI agents
- From AI Pilots to Agents:** The transition from traditional AI systems to more sophisticated AI agents marks a significant evolution in how financial services operate. Unlike black-box models like large language models (LLMs), AI agents provide transparent, verifiable results that can be traced back to their origins, ensuring accountability and regulatory compliance. Additionally, these agents can be controlled by humans through access controls, allowing for oversight and intervention when necessary.
- Traceability and Auditing:** One key advantage of AI agents over LLMs is their ability to produce traceable, auditable outputs. This feature enhances transparency and reduces potential risks associated with opaque algorithms. By maintaining a clear audit trail, stakeholders can verify the accuracy and reliability of the data generated by AI agents, which is crucial in industries where trust is paramount.
- Regulation and Human Oversight:** The integration of AI agents into asset management involves stringent regulation to ensure they meet ethical standards and operational requirements. Access controls enable organizations to manage who has access to specific AI agent functionalities, while incorporating human-in-the-loop mechanisms allows for immediate feedback and decision-making support, enhancing overall system security and effectiveness. These measures collectively contribute to making AI agents a reliable tool in managing complex financial assets.

## Agentic AI vs Generative AI: Key Differences Explained
- Agentic AI** refers to AI agents that autonomously take actions to fulfill specific goals without being explicitly programmed for each task.
- Generative AI**, on the other hand, generates novel outputs such as text or images based on patterns learned from existing data.
- These differences highlight how agentic AI leverages generative models strategically within broader objectives rather than solely creating content.
- For instance, in Salesforce's AgentForce platform, agentic AI utilizes generative AI models to enhance user interactions and tasks efficiently. [Source](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/agentic-ai-vs-generative-ai/)
- This strategic approach allows agentic AI to adapt and perform more complex functions effectively compared to purely generative systems.
